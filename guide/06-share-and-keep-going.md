## Make it yours

**Fork** copies the example you are looking at into **My projects**, where your edits are saved in your browser and stay yours. Give it a name in the box next to the picker. The original is untouched, and **Restore example** brings back an example's own version whenever you want.

## Share it

**Share** copies a link that carries the whole scene and script. Whoever opens it gets your project in their workbench. Because a script is code, a project that arrives from a link **is not run** until its reader has had a chance to read the script and press **Run the script**.

## Test an idea

**Sweep** runs the same scene many times with different slider values side by side and shows them as tiles, so you can see the effect of a number without moving it back and forth. Click a tile to continue from that run.

## Keep going

- The **Gallery** has an example of each kind of world.
- The **Blog** tells how some of the examples were made, including what went wrong.
- The script reference is `docs/script-api.md` in the repository, and the standard library (vectors, colors, bounds, neural nets, fields) is documented in `docs/stdlib.md`.
- The source is on GitHub. The principle behind the design is on the About page: *all models are wrong, some are useful*. A simulation is a model, and breve is built to make it quick to change what you think is wrong.
