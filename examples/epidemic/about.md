An [SIR epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compartmental_models_in_epidemiology) spreading through a network of people. Blue are **s**usceptible, red **i**nfected, green **r**ecovered (and now immune). Three people start infected.

### What to try
- **chance per sick neighbor**: how likely each sick neighbor is to pass it on, every tick.
- **ticks until recovered**: how long someone stays infectious.
- The chart shows the three groups over time. Watch how an outbreak that is just above the tipping point can still fizzle out.

The links are a [small-world network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small-world_network): mostly local, with a few long-range shortcuts that carry the disease across the map.
