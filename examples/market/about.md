A tiny economy on a network. Producers make goods, everyone eventually uses one up, and traders buy from the cheapest neighbor they are linked to. Each trade happens whole or not at all, and prices adapt to what people will pay.

### What to try
- **a producer makes a good every n ticks** against **everyone uses a good every n ticks**: supply and demand.
- **wages** and **running costs** decide who can afford to keep going.
- **most anyone will pay** caps prices.

Watch who runs out of cash, and whether goods end up where they are needed. Trade only happens along links, as in [networks of exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_network).
