[Conway's Game of Life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway%27s_Game_of_Life), moved into three dimensions. Every cell is alive or dead, and each tick a cell looks at its neighbors: a dead cell with the right number of live neighbors is born, and a live cell with too few or too many dies.

Most starts die out or fill the box; a few make shapes that grow, move and pulse. **starting density** is how crowded it begins (press **Reset** to apply it). The birth and survival counts are in the script, so edit them in the editor to explore other rules. Drag to turn the view.
