A few kinds of dots and **one table** that says which kinds pull toward which. Every dot feels the dots near it: very close ones push it away, and beyond that each kind is drawn toward or driven from each other kind by the amount in the table. The table is not symmetric (a red dot can chase a green one while the green runs away), and that is where the life comes from: clumps that behave like cells, orbits, chases, chains and membranes, all from nothing but pulls.

### What to try
- The slider *who attracts whom* picks a new random table with every number. Flip through a few; some make nothing, some make small creatures.
- *how mutual the attractions are* at 1 makes every pull two-way (dots just clump); lower it and chasing starts.
- *personal space* and *how far particles feel each other* change the size of the cells. *friction* settles or excites them.
- Open the editor: the whole rule is one short function in `life.js`, and the table comes from the function `attraction`. Write your own table.
