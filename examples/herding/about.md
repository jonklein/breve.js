You are the sheepdog. Drive it with **W A S D** (click the simulation first) and herd the sheep. Sheep flock together and run from the dog.

### What to try
- **how scared of the dog** and **how much sheep stick together** decide how they react: scared, loose sheep scatter; clingy ones move as a block.
- **dogSpeed** sets how fast you go.
- Try to herd them into one group and hold it.

The dog and sheep are two sets of agents in one world; sheep sense the dog across sets.
