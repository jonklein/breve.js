Two imaginary chemicals share a sheet. **u** is a food that is fed in everywhere. **v** eats u, makes more of itself, and slowly breaks down. Both spread out, u faster than v. That is enough to grow spots, stripes, mazes and coral: this is the [Gray-Scott model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction%E2%80%93diffusion_system), a classic of pattern formation (Turing's idea of how animals get their markings).

### What to try
- Press the **Pattern** buttons: each sets *feed* and *kill* to a known setting.
- **feed** is how fast food is added; **kill** is how fast v is removed. Small changes flip the pattern from spots to stripes.
- **how fast u / v spreads** sets how fine the pattern is.
- **Palette** only changes colors. The color shows how much v is in each cell.
- *starting blobs* and the seed only take effect after **Reset**.
