Bodies and brains evolve together, after [Karl Sims](https://www.karlsims.com/evolved-virtual-creatures.html)'s evolved virtual creatures. A population of walkers runs side by side as sub-simulations; a parent program scores how far each travels, keeps the best, and breeds mutated copies for the next generation.

### What to try
- **creatures per generation** and **ticks per generation** set how much search happens each round.
- **survivors kept** are copied unchanged; **mutation strength** is how much children differ.
- **reward staying upright** adds a bonus for not falling over.
- The strength, torque and joint speed limits apply from the next generation.
- Give it a few generations before judging: early walkers mostly fall over.
